Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Brunswick Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Brunswick to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Brunswick

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
